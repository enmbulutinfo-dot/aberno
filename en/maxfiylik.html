Privacy policy

How the information you send through our website is used and protected.

Effective date: 1 October 2026

1. General provisions

This policy sets out how personal data received through the aberno.uz website (the "Website") is processed. The data is processed by the Aberno Group companies: Aberno Butters Group LLC and ENM INFIN LLC (72 Uysozlar St, Yashnabad district, Tashkent, Uzbekistan).

This policy is prepared in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data".

2. What data we receive

  • Through the contact form: your name, phone number, company name, country, subject and message.
  • Through the price request form: your name, phone number and order parameters (grammage, plies, width, volume, country).

We do not ask for passport details, payment card details or passwords.

3. Why we use the data

  • To reply to your request and get in touch with you.
  • To prepare a commercial offer and price calculation.
  • To communicate about partnerships, supplies and job openings.

Your data is not used for marketing messages without your consent.

4. Legal basis

By submitting a form, you consent to the processing of your data for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Retention period

Data is kept until your request has been handled or until you withdraw your consent, and is protected in accordance with the laws of Uzbekistan.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. The only exception is requests from authorised government bodies in cases provided for by law.

7. Cookies and technical data

The Website does not use cookies. Your selected theme (light or dark) is stored only in your browser and is never sent to us.

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and map data from jsDelivr. These services may receive technical data (such as your IP address) needed to load the page.

8. Your rights

  • To know how your data is processed.
  • To request correction of inaccurate data.
  • To request deletion of your data and withdraw your consent.

To make a request: info@aberno.uz, +998 95 342-70-70.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The new version is published on this page and takes effect from the date of publication.
