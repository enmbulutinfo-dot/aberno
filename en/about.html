About Aberno

Founded in 2021, the company now works in two areas: hygiene products and edible fats.

Who we are

A reliable manufacturer of everyday essentials

Aberno was founded in Uzbekistan in 2021. We specialise in products that households use every day.

Under the BULUT brand we produce paper napkins and wet wipes, boxed tissues, paper towels and toilet paper, as well as dispensers for HoReCa. Under the MARGARITTO and SMAYLO brands we produce margarine and spreads.

Today our team has more than 150 employees, and the BULUT brand is rapidly expanding its exports to Central Asian markets.
