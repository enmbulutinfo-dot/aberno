Who we are

A reliable manufacturer of everyday essentials

Aberno Group was founded in Uzbekistan in 2021. Under the BULUT brand we produce paper napkins and wet wipes, boxed tissues, paper towels, toilet paper, HoReCa dispensers and sanitary base paper.

Aberno Butters Group produces margarine, spreads and rendered vegetable fat under the MARGARITTO and SMAYLO brands. Our portfolio includes up to 30 SKUs, and the range keeps growing.

Today our team has more than 150 employees.

Milestones
